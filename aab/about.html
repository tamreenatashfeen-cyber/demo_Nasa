The name

Blue is water. Atlas After Blue maps what remains after the water has passed: a flood's aftermath, seen from space. Shortened, the name becomes AAB, and Aab is the Urdu word for water. The full name describes what the site does. The short form carries where it comes from.

Who this is for

  • PDMA KP
  • Provincial Emergency Operation Centre

AAB is for provincial disaster teams such as PDMA KP and the Provincial Emergency Operation Centre, who review satellite-derived observations next to situation reports and damage records.

Why it fits

Free, open data.

NISAR is a NASA–ISRO mission whose data is public, so no commercial imagery budget is needed.

Sees through cloud.

Radar works through monsoon cloud and at night, when optical imagery often cannot.

A rule you can explain.

The method is a transparent threshold, not a black box, so a team can defend it in a briefing.

Honest about uncertainty.

Every result is labelled candidate, Low confidence, with what it cannot prove.

AAB shows how NASA's open radar data can support a small team in Pakistan. It is supplementary evidence and does not replace field assessment.
